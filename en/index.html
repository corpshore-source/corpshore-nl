From the VOC to the Digital Era — Dutch Operational Excellence Redefined.

The Dutch East India Company — founded in Amsterdam in 1602 — was the world's first distributed operational multinational: shareholders in Amsterdam, operations in Batavia, suppliers across Asia and clients throughout Europe. All coordinated remotely, without real-time communication. Corpshore does the same in the 21st century — 18 delivery hubs, 35+ languages, one accountable point of contact.

The Dutch business tradition is built on polder-model thinking: consensus, efficiency, and the ability to bring complex interests together into workable solutions. That same mentality drives our approach to outsourcing. We do not build generic service contracts — we design operational models that fit precisely how your organisation works.

With delivery hubs in 18 countries, capacity in 35+ languages, and an independent ranking of #2 BPO in Europe (Outsource Accelerator 2026), we bring global scale to Dutch quality standards. For organisations that demand precision, compliance and reliability, Corpshore Netherlands is the obvious choice.
